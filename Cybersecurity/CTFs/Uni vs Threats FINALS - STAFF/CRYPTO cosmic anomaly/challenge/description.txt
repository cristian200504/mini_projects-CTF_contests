Title: Cosmic Anomaly

Six thousand four hundred ninety‑two hours after the crash, it was discovered that an unidentified spacecraft had crash‑landed in an isolated sector of the desert. Our recovery teams secured the perimeter, but the vessel's main databanks were heavily damaged upon impact. We managed to extract a single file from the wreckage.

At first glance, it looks like a massive string of random characters. However, our analysts suspect this isn't just meaningless alien linguistics—there is a deliberate, underlying stenographic structure to this data anomaly.

We need your expertise to peel back the layers of this payload. Can you analyze the anomaly and recover the true alien message?
