Title: Signal Inteference

Transmission Incoming...
"Captain, we are receiving an emergency transmission from Mars Base Alpha. However, our comms arrays are picking up a massive anomaly. An unknown hidden extraterrestrial frequency is overlapping with the human transmission, filling the log with alien garbage data.

Mars Base Alpha relies on strict, standardized communication protocols for its emergency beacons, while the alien broadcast appears to be entirely chaotic, unstructured noise.

We need you to write a script to filter out the alien interference and recover the base signal. The fate of the mission depends on it!"
