hard
December 31st, 1999. 11:58 PM.

While scanning forgotten subnets from the Millennium Systems network, you stumble upon a relic: a web-based MSN Messenger client, still running after all these years.

The chat service seems operational. A bot named SmarterChild_Y2K responds to messages, and contacts from the old days are still listed — including the mysterious epoch_admin.

Somewhere in this ancient messenger, classified communications are archived — encrypted, waiting for someone clever enough to find and decrypt them.

A diagnostic tool from the era is still running on the server. It was hastily written in C and never properly secured.

Three fragments, three different skills:

The ciphertext sleeps in old network traffic.
The IV hides in plain sight.
The key lives in volatile memory.
Combine all three to decrypt the final message and uncover the truth about Project Epoch.

** - Constraints** - No brute-force - Pay close attention to server responses and headers - The binary runs server-side — you interact with it via HTTP - This is a 2-part challenge (multi-category)
